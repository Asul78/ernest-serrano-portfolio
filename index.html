I'm a Computer Engineering graduate from Laguna State Polytechnic University who found his place where design meets the technical side of the web. What started as an interest in building interfaces turned into a day-to-day practice of shaping, maintaining, and improving websites at scale.

At eClerx Philippines, I've worked across more than 400 client websites, building new ones, redesigning others, and handling change requests on the Duda platform. That volume taught me to care about consistency, accuracy, and quality assurance as much as about how a page looks.

These days my focus has shifted toward search: publishing and optimizing content, improving organic visibility, and keeping site quality high. I still design, still write markup, and still enjoy the small details that make a site feel finished.
